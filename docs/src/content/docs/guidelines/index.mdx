---
title: The twelve laws
description: How every Herefront screen should feel — in plain words.
---

Our references: **Apple** for how a control looks and behaves, **Revolut** for the path to the goal and the home
screen, **Telegram** for speed. When they disagree, the role whose reference it is decides.

1. **One main action.** One filled button in view. The rest are quieter.
2. **Complex → one tap.** Glue steps into one button that does what the person meant.
3. **Do, don't ask.** Act at once and offer “Undo”. Confirm only what cannot be undone.
4. **Smart defaults.** Remember the last choice; fill what we already know.
5. **Show a little, reveal the rest.** Most tasks on the surface, rare ones a tap deeper.
6. **Instant.** Answer a tap within 100 ms; show a spinner only after 150 ms.
7. **Every state is designed.** Empty, loading, error, no access, too many — each says what to do next.
8. **Words are interface.** A verb and an object on a button. No jargon.
9. **Fingers and keyboard.** 44 pt targets; Esc closes, Enter confirms.
10. **A component like Apple's.** Same shape, same press, same motion.
11. **Quiet and systematic.** Only tokens, one scale, motion with meaning, reduced motion respected.
12. **No layout shift.** What is above your eyes never moves. New things appear below or in a pinned place; a status is
    one line that changes in place; heights ease over about 200 ms; we scroll for you only when you are already at the bottom.
