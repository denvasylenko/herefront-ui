## Rows

- **Title** on the left; on the right a grey **value**, a **chevron** (opens a deeper screen), or a control such as a [toggle](../toggle/).
- A chevron means “opens a screen”. No chevron means an action or plain data.
- Separators are hairlines inset from the left; the last row has none.
- A row with `href` is a link; with `nav` and no `href` it is a button — both highlight while pressed.
